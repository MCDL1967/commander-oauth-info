Commander privacy information

Updated October 6, 2026. Commander is a private personal application for its owner’s own Google account.

Google account access

When authorized, Commander uses Gmail read access to find and read messages, threads, labels and drafts relevant to the owner’s requests. It uses Gmail send access only after the owner reviews and approves an exact message. The owner can revoke access through their Google Account permissions.

How information is used

Relevant email text and conversation context can be sent to Anthropic’s API to generate the requested response. Replies and approved previews are delivered through the owner’s paired Telegram conversation. These providers process information under their own applicable terms and data policies.

Storage and control

Account credentials, conversation history, working records and send receipts are stored privately on the owner’s computer. Local records remain until the owner removes them. Removing Google authorization stops future access but does not delete previously saved local records or provider-held records.

Commander does not sell personal information or use Gmail data for advertising. This informational website does not receive Gmail messages or account credentials.

Contact

The application owner can be contacted through the support email displayed on Commander’s Google authorization screen.
